Our Story

From Dream to Rolling Reality

Bar on the Move started with a vision: bring the premium bar experience directly to the people. No venue constraints. No tipping out the house. Just a sleek, fully-stocked mobile bar, a talented bartender, and an unforgettable atmosphere — wherever you need it in Delaware and beyond.

Our custom-built trailer features LED lighting, a full back bar with premium spirits, cocktail tables, and on-demand bartender staffing. Whether it's 20 guests or 2,000 — we scale to your event.

🍸
Full Premium Bar Setup
Spirits, wine, craft beers, cocktail mixers, glassware — all included. Customize your menu for any crowd.
👨‍🍳

Licensed, insured, and experienced bartenders matched to your event size and style.
